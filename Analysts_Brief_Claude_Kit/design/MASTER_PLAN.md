# Experience and situation

Kalinda is a fictional African republic, not a place inside a document. The Commission office and Tamarind Crossing school hall are locations within that world. The miniature is a teaching model of Kalinda. A disputed election, clashes at the Crossing, rumours of an army mutiny and a corruption scandal put pressure on the National Dialogue, which opens Friday.

You are a junior analyst. You grew up near Tamarind Crossing and declare this connection. It is an interest, not a disqualification. Dr Amina Diallo directs the research unit. Hon. Grace Mwangi chairs the National Dialogue. Nana Efua’s recorded family recollection is an archive source, not an additional character quest.

The plain-language goal is: **check what can be trusted, test what changed events, recommend what to do, explain your reasoning.**

## Briefing design
P02 first records the role-based connection. It then delivers Diallo’s original voice note with a concise visible summary. Full transcript is one tap away. Preserve the original spoken line in `production/MEDIA_PLAN.md`; the shorter visual text is a summary, not a replacement of source facts. No compulsory video, extra tutorial quiz or character tour.

Opening visible summary: “Kalinda faces a disputed election, clashes at the Crossing, rumours of an army mutiny and a corruption scandal. Check the evidence, test the causes, then recommend a response.”

Transition to model: “Wednesday · Test the explanations.” The orb introduces the three circulating explanations from the SDD, then the learner explores the base history and seven case notes. This fits within P05, not a new assessment.

Hall briefing summary: “Two district boundaries have changed. Residents fear weaker representation; officials cite new population figures. Test a response we can explain and carry out.” A compact Details control carries the journalist, rally, contractor allegation and barracks rumours. The source press reaction plays according to political climate.

## Flow and pacing
| Stage | Main action | Required work | Intended pacing, to validate |
|---|---|---|---|
| Arrival | Enter role | Name, declaration, situation briefing | About 1 minute |
| Evidence | Read and tag | 16 sourced pieces plus 2 claims; source threshold retained | About 5–7 minutes |
| Kalinda | Test and explain | Seven factors available; base timeline, comparisons, up to 3 findings and a sentence | About 4–6 minutes |
| Hall | Choose a response | Five choices, test/revise, rationale | About 4–5 minutes |
| Chair | Communicate | Record about 60 seconds OR type | About 2–3 minutes |
| Debrief | Understand consequences | Three strands and forward view; optional deeper reflection | About 1–2 minutes |

The source estimates 20–25 minutes, untimed. These are pacing targets, not a claim from learner testing. Keep the source content intact; shortening the number of evidence pieces would be a substantive curriculum change. Do not achieve brevity by rushing the reader. Motion contributes well under a minute when repeated durations are minimized.

## Presentation principles
One persistent app canvas, changing states. No route reload between documents. Environment dominates. Small context-specific composer appears only when action is needed; it folds and returns to the upper-right dock while the learner reads or watches a result. No default chat transcript covering the scene. Previous messages stay in Notes.

The user’s final first-person requirement supersedes earlier detached isometric office proposals. Start at standing eye level within the office, settle at seated eye level, then inspect objects by moving/tilting the objects. A gentle gaze downward is allowed; camera travel outside the room is not.

Only two character portraits are needed: Diallo and Mwangi. Nana can be audio and transcript only. Hall attendees in generated concepts are optional environmental dressing, not required animated characters. The first release may use off-camera murmur with empty visible foreground seats.
