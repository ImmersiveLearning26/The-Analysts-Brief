# Opener: three storylines

All three are 20–25 s, 1920×1080 at 30 fps, with no voiceover. They use the game's own 3D models (office, Kalinda map, hall, chair office) and the AI globe, with Afacad and Manrope type and the orange accent rationed. Music is a quiet bed from the free/local library, with sound on visible actions.

**Miva Open University** (required by the SDD's standard Miva shell: "Miva University opening → optional intro sequence → title/landing"): every storyline opens with a 2–3 s Miva Open University ident, using the official logo carried in and landing with weight. The end card says "Miva Open University · IRS 103". Use the official logo file; never redraw it.

## A. "One question, four rooms" (recommended: it's the learner's real journey, so a stranger follows it)
**Device:** the object is the explanation. **Form:** one continuous take, a flight through one world.
The globe asks a single question, and the camera dives into the case file on the office desk, then out through the floor into Kalinda, then into the hall, then up to the Chair's evening office. Each room is one verb.
- **First 2 s:** warm white. The globe glows centre frame and the line "Kalinda is in crisis" types in beside it. The office cutaway rises underneath.
- **Beats:** hook (globe + line) → *Read* (the folder opens, a sheet slides out, a blue O lands) → *Test* (the floor drops into the Kalinda map turning indigo, vehicles move) → *Respond* (the hall table, a pin glows) → *Brief* (the chair office at dusk, the recorder light) → the globe docks into the title → end card "The Analyst's Brief" with the line "Check the evidence. Test the causes. Brief the Chair."
- **Look:** warm white #f4f3ee for the office and hall, Orizon indigo for Kalinda, dusk for the chair office. Afacad titles, a tan tracked kicker per room (THE EVIDENCE / THE MODEL / THE HALL / THE BRIEFING).
- **Sound:** a soft piano and pulse bed, a paper slide, a stamp tick, a low swell on the indigo drop, and a single chime on the title.

## B. "The headline that changes"
**Device:** a question with the input inside it. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds.
A newspaper headline reads "Disputed election. Army mutiny rumour." The globe pulls one word at a time out of it, and each word becomes the 3D place it names (election → the radio tower on Kalinda, army → the barracks, dispute → the hall). The headline rewrites itself into a calmer one.
- **First 2 s:** a full-frame headline in big type, with the globe sliding in under "mutiny".
- **Beats:** headline → three words become three places → the camera pulls back to the whole Kalinda island → the headline rewrites → title.
- **Look:** newsprint-off-white type field cutting into the indigo map; the accent only on the changed word.
- **Sound:** a typewriter-free paper rustle, a bass hit per word, a riser into the rewrite.

## C. "The globe's eye" (the bold one)
**Device:** a character does the work. **Form:** an endless zoom.
The globe is the camera. It drifts down onto the office desk, its reflection lands on the case file, and it dives into the folder's map page, which becomes real 3D Kalinda. It falls further into a building and comes out in the hall, then pulls back until everything is a tiny model in the globe's glass sphere.
- **First 2 s:** extreme close-up of the globe's strands, filling the frame, then pulling back to reveal it hovering over a desk.
- **Beats:** strands → desk → map page → Kalinda → hall → everything inside the sphere → title.
- **Look:** dark-to-warm transitions, glass refraction on the sphere, type kept minimal (title only).
- **Sound:** an airy hum that drops in pitch on each zoom, ending in a clean tone.
